What this trial does and does not establish

Finalized pool structures, mint controls and vault balances are checked on chain. Pool activity comes from DexScreener. Sampled non-vault holdings are lower bounds, not complete holder distribution. Fees, slippage and fills are modeled. No wallet is connected and no real orders are enabled.

These are deterministic proxy experiments. Their results do not validate the original strategies, prove trader skill, represent Grok/JEV approval, or authorize live trading. Original ledgers and gates remain separate.

Each account starts its own seven-day window. Maximum three positions, roughly 1% equity per entry, 5% daily loss halt, 20% stop, 30% take profit, six-hour time stop. Fresh data is required to mark or exit. Stale positions remain unresolved.

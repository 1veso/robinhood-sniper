# RUG DESK

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
The owner supervising an automatic meme-token paper-trading experiment on an always-on Windows PC.

## Product Purpose
Make real recorded decisions, holdings, costs, market observations and operational failures visible while the existing paper workers run independently.

## Operating Context
Robinhood wallet-following is the active seven-day exploratory account. Solana RUG DESK is a separate setup-blocked account. The user explicitly requested a trading-console interface, bot selection, technical analysis, traded coins, position sizes and P&L, and delegated selection of important information.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The observation console is read-only and local. It must not change account identity, trading rules, original deadline, paid-API restrictions or ledger records. Paper fills are simulations using market observations, not signed market transactions. Automatic live execution remains unimplemented. No manual-trading controls. Acceptance accounts are excluded from strategy performance. Unknown safety and stale observations must remain visible. Future bots require explicit account adapters; results are never blended.

## Evidence on Hand
Installed SQLite ledgers, recorded pinned-pool quotes, decision records, trial manifests, worker status and Telegram delivery acknowledgements. No complete exchange tick/volume feed exists.

## Product Principles
Truth before appearance. Distinguish data freshness from page refresh. Preserve each account's identity. Show uncertainty next to the number it affects.
